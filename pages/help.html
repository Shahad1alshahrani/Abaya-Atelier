Help & FAQ

If you have questions about our abayas or your order, you may find the answers below.

How can I place an order?

Browse our collection, choose your favorite abaya, and click Add to Cart. Then proceed to checkout to complete your order.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery usually takes between 2–5 business days inside Saudi Arabia.

Can I return or exchange an abaya?

Yes, returns and exchanges are available within 7 days if the item is unused and in its original condition.
